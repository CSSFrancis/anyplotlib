:meth:`~anyplotlib.Plot2D.set_scalebar_style` recolours the automatic scale
bar.  It was hardcoded white-on-a-translucent-dark-pill, which is unreadable
over a light image and could not be matched to a house style.  ``color`` sets
the bar and its label; ``bgcolor`` sets the pill, or ``"none"`` to drop the
pill entirely.
