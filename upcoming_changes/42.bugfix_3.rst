The 1-D y-axis label is no longer drawn through the tick numbers.  Its x was a
fixed fraction of the left gutter, on the assumption that this cleared the
ticks whatever their width; a string like ``100000`` reaches back past it and
the rotated label was struck through the numbers.  The label is now placed
relative to the widest tick string, and clamped so it stays on the canvas.

.. note::
   A tick string wide enough to fill the gutter on its own (8 characters, e.g.
   ``-5.6e-17``) still leaves nowhere clear for the label — it goes as far
   left as it can and clips the leading characters.  Fixing that needs a wider
   left gutter, and ``PAD_L`` is shared across panels to keep their plot areas
   aligned.
